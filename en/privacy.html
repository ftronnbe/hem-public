Privacy policy

Effective from October 1, 2026. Updated October 16, 2026.

Matro is an app for the household: a shopping list, the week's food, recipes and expenses that you share with each other. This policy says what the app stores about you, why, where it is kept and how you delete it. Most things are the same on iPhone and Android; where something differs, it says what applies if you use Matro on Android.

Contact

Send questions about your data to team.app.consortium{at}gmail{dot}com.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and your name, which you get from Apple or Google when you sign in, and which way you sign in.
  • What you add: homes and who is in them, shopping lists, the week's food, workouts, recipes with photos, expenses and who paid, ratings of dishes, choices in Weekly picks.
  • Profile picture: if you take one. It is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, and only the phones of the people in your homes have the keys that open it. We can't see the picture, and neither can Supabase, which stores it.
  • Workouts: the workouts you add to the week: what kind, which day, a goal if you give one (distance or time) and who is taking part. The app reads nothing from Apple Health or other fitness apps and measures nothing itself.
  • Food preferences: diet (for example vegetarian) and what you avoid (for example gluten, nuts or cilantro). This is only stored if you choose to fill it in, and you can remove it whenever you like.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, a code for your phone (an APNs token on iPhone, an FCM token on Android) and whether it is an iPhone or an Android phone. On Android we also store which language you use the app in, so that the notification can be written in your language. And the notifications that have been sent to you.
  • The Sous-chef and Matro Plus: how many tasks the home has given the Sous-chef (Matro's AI features) per month and of what kind (for example a guide or a question), which tasks the home has earned, and if you buy Matro Plus: the purchase's ID at Apple or, on Android, the purchase's code at Google Play (purchase token), which subscription, its status, until when it is valid and whether it is in a free trial. The content of the tasks is not stored for this.
  • The Sous-chef's calls on Android: on Android, the Sous-chef's tasks go through our server. For each call a short log is kept: who made it, in which home, what kind of task, whether it used one of the home's tasks, whether it worked and how big it was (number of tokens). What you asked and what the Sous-chef answered are never stored, neither there nor in the server's logs.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes or hangs, it sends a technical report: where in the code it happened, the app's version, the system version (iOS or Android) and the phone model. The report contains nothing you have added and is not linked to you or your account.

We don't collect your location, your contacts or anything for advertising, and we use no analytics or tracking service.

Camera, face and microphone

  • The profile picture is taken with a smile. When you take it, the app uses the front camera and the phone's face tracking (ARKit on iPhone) to see how much you are smiling. This only happens on the phone and only while the camera is open: no face data is stored, sent anywhere or used for anything else. The only thing stored is the picture you choose to use.
  • The camera is also used when you take a photo of a recipe or a dish. The photo is only stored if you save the recipe.
  • Say your list: when you speak items into the list, the audio is interpreted on the phone. Neither audio nor text is sent anywhere; what is stored is the items that end up on the list.

Why

  • So that the app works: to show the same list, week and recipes to everyone in the home and to work out who owes what. This is the basis for almost everything we store (contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Food preferences are stored to choose suggestions that suit the home. What you avoid is stored with your consent (Article 9(2)(a)). You give it by choosing something, and withdraw it by unselecting it.
  • Notifications are only sent if you have turned them on.
  • The check with Google Play Integrity, the limit on how many calls to the Sous-chef one person can make and the log of those calls are there to protect the service from abuse (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f)).

Who sees what

  • What you add to a home is seen by the others in the same home: lists, the week, workouts, recipes, expenses, ratings and food preferences. No one outside the home sees it.
  • Your profile picture is seen by the people in any of your homes. If someone leaves a home, the home's key is changed, so that they can't open new pictures from it.
  • Ratings of dishes in the Cookbook are added up to an average rating that all users see, without names.
  • We never sell data and only share it with the services needed to run the app (see below).

Where the data is kept

  • Supabase stores the database and the photos and runs our server, within the EU (Ireland). The profile pictures are stored there encrypted.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, notifications to iPhones and, if you use them, Apple's AI features for reading recipes. With Apple's Private Cloud Compute the text only leaves the phone to be interpreted and is not stored.
  • Anthropic (USA) reads recipes, writes guides and answers questions for the Sous-chef, and only if you have allowed it. On iPhone this applies to phones that don't have Apple Intelligence turned on, and the phone sends the task straight to Anthropic. On Android all of the Sous-chef's tasks go to Anthropic through our server at Supabase, which passes them on without storing their content. The recipe's text and photos are then sent there to be read. Anthropic processes them on our behalf, not to train its models, and the transfer to the USA is covered by the EU's standard contractual clauses.
  • Google handles sign-in if you sign in with Google.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications to Android phones. Google then gets the phone's notification code and the notification's content: title, text and sometimes a photo of the dish. The text is written on our server, in the language you use the app in.
  • Google Play Integrity checks on Android that calls to the Sous-chef come from the genuine Matro app from Google Play on a genuine phone. The app asks Google for a proof (integrity token) for that very call, and our server has Google read it. Google produces the answer from information about the app, the phone and whether the app was installed from Google Play. Google doesn't get the call's content, only a check value (hash) of it, and we don't store the answer.
  • Payment for a subscription is handled by the Apple App Store on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We never see your card details. Our server reads the purchase's status straight from Apple or Google to give the home Matro Plus.

How long

  • For as long as you keep your account.
  • A profile picture you replace or remove is deleted right away.
  • Things you remove are gone for good after 90 days. They are kept that long so that phones that have been offline have time to see that they were removed.
  • Sent notifications are removed after 30 days.
  • The log of the Sous-chef's calls through our server is removed after two days.
  • Crash reports are removed after six months.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed right away (see below).

Your rights

  • See and download your data: Settings → Account → Download my data gives you everything we have about you as a file.
  • Change: most things you change directly in the app. Other things we change if you get in touch.
  • Remove the profile picture: Settings → tap yourself → Remove photo.
  • Delete your account: see below.
  • Withdraw consent: unselect what you avoid under Settings → Food.
  • Complain: you can always turn to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se, or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

Delete your account

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, your name, your profile picture, your food preferences, ratings, swipes and own recipes are removed. Homes where only you are a member are removed with all their content. In homes you share with others, what you have done stays without your name, so that lists and expenses add up for the others, and your recipes there are taken over by someone in the home. If you signed in with Apple, we also remove the app's link to your Apple Account.
  • Without the app: if you no longer have the app, you can ask us to delete your Matro account by emailing team.app.consortium{at}gmail{dot}com from the email address you sign in with. We delete it in the same way within a month.
  • Matro Plus: a subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account. Cancel it first, in your Apple Account settings or under Subscriptions in Google Play.

Changes

If the policy changes in a way that matters to you, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
