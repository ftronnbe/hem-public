Subscription

Matro Plus is a subscription for the whole home.

  • The Sous-chef is Matro's AI features. Every home gets a number of tasks for the Sous-chef at no cost (a new Cook now guide, a question about a recipe or a recipe the Sous-chef reads): 10 to start with and 5 new ones every month, and more that are earned in the app. The number and the ways to earn more may change.
  • With Matro Plus the Sous-chef's tasks are unlimited for everyone in the home, within a reasonable limit for normal use (currently 300 a month). Plus applies in up to three homes the person who pays is a member of: first the one where Plus was bought or to which it has been moved, then the others in the order that person joined them. If the person who pays leaves the home, Plus stops applying there.
  • Without Matro Plus a home has room for two people, and each of them can create a home of their own and join homes others have created. With Matro Plus the home has room for up to 12 people, and the person who pays can create more homes. Anyone who is already a member may stay.
  • Without Matro Plus each person can save 15 recipes of their own (from links, photos or written by hand); the Cookbook's dishes are always free. The shopping list's history is part of Matro Plus.
  • A free trial turns into a paid subscription automatically unless you cancel it before the trial ends.
  • The person who pays can give a free month to a friend. The month is redeemed in the App Store, only applies to someone who hasn't had Matro Plus before, and turns into a paid subscription unless the friend cancels it.
  • The subscription is bought and paid for through the Apple App Store and follows Apple's terms for purchases.
  • It renews automatically for the same period until you cancel it. You cancel it in the settings for your Apple Account, at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Refunds are requested from Apple.
  • If the price changes, you will be notified according to Apple's rules before the new price applies.
